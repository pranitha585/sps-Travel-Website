Munnar

Kerala's Picturesque Hill Station

Munnar is a picturesque hill station located in the Idukki district of Kerala, India. Known for its stunning landscapes, lush tea plantations, and cool climate, Munnar is a popular tourist destination.

Geography and Climate

Location

Munnar is situated in the Western Ghats at an altitude of about 1,600 meters (5,200 feet) above sea level.

Climate

The climate is generally cool and pleasant throughout the year, with temperatures ranging from 10°C to 25°C. The monsoon season, from June to September, brings heavy rainfall.

Attractions

Tea Plantations

Vast expanses of tea gardens are a hallmark of Munnar. Visitors can tour tea factories to learn about tea processing.

Eravikulam National Park

Home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr, this park is also famous for the Neelakurinji flower, which blooms once every 12 years.

Mattupetty Dam

A popular spot for boating and picnicking, offering scenic views of the surrounding hills and forests.

Anamudi Peak

The highest peak in South India, located within the Eravikulam National Park, is a favorite among trekkers.

Top Station

The highest point on the Munnar-Kodaikanal road, offering panoramic views of the Western Ghats.

Echo Point

A scenic spot where one can hear the echo of sounds reverberating through the hills.

Activities

  • Trekking: Munnar offers numerous trekking trails, including routes to Anamudi Peak and Chokramudi Peak.
  • Boating: Available at Mattupetty Dam and Kundala Lake.
  • Tea Tasting: Visitors can sample different varieties of tea at local tea factories.
  • Spice Tours: Explore the spice plantations and learn about the cultivation of spices like cardamom, cinnamon, and pepper.

Best Time to Visit

October to March April to May June to September

Munnar's natural beauty, combined with its rich history and cultural heritage, makes it a must-visit destination for nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts alike.

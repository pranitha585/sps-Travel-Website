Coorg

Scotland of India

Coorg, officially known as Kodagu, is a serene and picturesque district in the state of Karnataka, India. Renowned for its lush coffee plantations, misty hills, and rich cultural heritage, Coorg is often referred to as the "Scotland of India."

Geography and Climate

Location

Coorg is located in the Western Ghats, at an altitude ranging from 900 to 1,750 meters (3,000 to 5,740 feet) above sea level.

Climate

The climate is pleasant throughout the year, with temperatures ranging from 10°C to 25°C. The monsoon season, from June to September, brings heavy rainfall.

Attractions

Abbey Falls

A stunning waterfall located amidst coffee plantations and spice estates, offering a picturesque view.

Raja's Seat

A popular viewpoint offering panoramic views of the lush valleys and misty hills. It is a favorite spot for watching sunsets.

Talakaveri

The source of the River Kaveri, located in the Brahmagiri Hills. It is considered a sacred site and has a temple dedicated to Lord Agastheeswara.

Madikeri Fort

A historical fort built in the 17th century, housing a museum, a temple, and a chapel.

Iruppu Falls

A scenic waterfall located in the Brahmagiri Range, considered sacred and associated with the Ramayana.

Activities

  • Trekking: Popular trekking routes include Tadiandamol, the highest peak in Coorg, and Brahmagiri Peak.
  • White Water Rafting: Available on the Barapole River, offering an adventurous experience.
  • Bird Watching: Coorg is a haven for bird watchers, with numerous species of birds found in the region.
  • Plantation Tours: Visitors can explore coffee and spice plantations, learning about the cultivation and processing of these crops.

Best Time to Visit

October to March April to May June to September

Coorg's natural beauty, rich cultural heritage, and pleasant climate make it a favorite destination for tourists seeking a peaceful retreat in the hills.

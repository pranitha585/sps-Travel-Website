Kodaikanal

Princess of Hill Stations

Kodaikanal, often referred to as the "Princess of Hill Stations," is a charming hill station located in the Dindigul district of Tamil Nadu, India. Known for its cool climate, lush greenery, and serene lakes.

Geography and Climate

Location

Kodaikanal is situated in the Palani Hills of the Western Ghats at an altitude of about 2,133 meters (7,000 feet) above sea level.

Climate

The climate is cool and pleasant throughout the year, with temperatures ranging from 8°C to 20°C. The monsoon season, from June to September, brings moderate to heavy rainfall.

Attractions

Kodaikanal Lake

A star-shaped man-made lake, it is the centerpiece of the town and offers boating facilities. The lake is surrounded by a 5-kilometer-long paved path ideal for walking.

Pillar Rocks

Three giant granite boulders standing vertically, offering a stunning view of the surrounding valleys. The area also features a mini-garden and viewpoints.

Green Valley View (Suicide Point)

A viewpoint offering breathtaking views of the lush green valleys and the Vaigai Dam. The name "Suicide Point" is a misnomer and refers to its steep cliffs.

Silver Cascade Falls

A beautiful waterfall located on the Ghat road leading to Kodaikanal, formed by the overflow of Kodaikanal Lake.

Kurinji Andavar Temple

A temple dedicated to Lord Murugan, it is famous for the Kurinji flower, which blooms once every 12 years.

Activities

  • Boating: Available at Kodaikanal Lake, offering a peaceful experience on the water.
  • Trekking: Popular trekking routes include the trek to Dolphin's Nose, Pillar Rocks, and Vattakanal.
  • Cycling: The cool climate and scenic routes make cycling a popular activity.

Best Time to Visit

October to March April to May June to September

Kodaikanal's natural beauty, pleasant climate, and serene environment make it a favorite destination for tourists seeking a peaceful retreat in the hills.

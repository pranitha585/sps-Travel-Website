HILL STATIONS

A hill station is a town or settlement located at a higher elevation than the surrounding plains or valleys, often chosen for its cooler climate and scenic beauty. They are developed as summer retreats or tourist destinations, offering respite from the heat of the plains. Here are some stunning hill stations across India that you can visit:

OOTY

Queen of Hill Stations - Tamil Nadu

Ooty, or Udhagamandalam, is a picturesque hill station in Tamil Nadu, India, nestled in the Nilgiri Hills. Known for its tea plantations, lush greenery, and pleasant climate.

KODAIKANAL

Princess of Hill Stations - Tamil Nadu

Kodaikanal, nestled in the hills of Tamil Nadu, is a charming hill station often called the "Princess of Hill Stations." Known for its misty landscapes, pristine lakes, and lush greenery.

COORG

Scotland of India - Karnataka

Coorg, or Kodagu, is a scenic hill station in Karnataka, known for its lush coffee plantations, misty landscapes, and vibrant culture. Often called the "Scotland of India."

MUNNAR

Kashmir of South India - Kerala

Munnar, located in Kerala, is a breathtaking hill station renowned for its sprawling tea plantations, misty hills, and serene valleys. Its cool climate and natural beauty make it a haven for trekkers and nature enthusiasts.

ARAKU VALLEY

Ooty of Andhra Pradesh - Andhra Pradesh

Araku Valley, located in Andhra Pradesh, is a stunning hill station known for its lush greenery, coffee plantations, and tribal culture. With its breathtaking landscapes and serene ambiance, it's a perfect escape for nature enthusiasts.

LONAVALA

City of Caves - Maharashtra

Lonavala, located in Maharashtra, is a picturesque hill station known for its lush greenery, serene lakes, and majestic waterfalls. Famous for its scenic beauty and proximity to Mumbai and Pune.

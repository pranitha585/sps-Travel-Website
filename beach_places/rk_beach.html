RK Beach

Visakhapatnam — Along the Bay of Bengal coastline, near Ramakrishna Mission

Ramakrishna Beach (RK Beach), named after the Ramakrishna Mission, is Visakhapatnam's vibrant waterfront destination. With its golden sands, scenic views, and iconic landmarks, it's the perfect blend of recreation, spirituality, and coastal charm.

Key Highlights

Iconic Landmarks

  • Submarine Museum — INS Kursura, real submarine turned museum
  • Victory at Sea War Memorial — Tribute to naval heroes
  • Kali Temple — Ancient temple near the shore
  • TU 142 Aircraft Museum — Naval aviation history

Beach Experiences

  • Sunrise views over Dolphin's Nose hill
  • Evening strolls along 3km promenade
  • Local street food — Corn, bajji, and sugarcane juice
  • Beach volleyball and cricket matches

Water Activities

  • Jet ski rides (seasonal)
  • Swimming in designated safe zones
  • Dolphin sightings (occasional)
  • Boat rides to nearby islands

Best Time to Visit

  • Early Morning: 5–7am (cool breeze, sunrise views)
  • Evening: 4–7pm (sunset, street food, activities)
  • Best Season: October to March (pleasant weather)
  • Avoid: Midday heat and monsoon season (June–September)

What Makes RK Beach Special

  • Home to India's first submarine museum (INS Kursura)
  • Perfect combination of beach recreation and historical sites
  • Well-maintained promenade with beautiful landscaping
  • Stunning views of the Eastern Ghats meeting the Bay of Bengal

Nearby Attractions

  • Kailasagiri (5km): Hilltop park with ropeway and panoramic views
  • VUDA Park (1km): Children's amusement park and musical fountain
  • Visakha Museum (2km): Local history and artifacts of Vizag
  • Rushikonda Beach (10km): Pristine sands and watersports hub

RK Beach perfectly captures Visakhapatnam's essence — where naval history meets coastal leisure, creating unforgettable experiences for visitors of all ages.

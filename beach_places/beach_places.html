BEACHES

Beaches are coastal landforms where the sea meets the shore, offering scenic beauty, recreational activities, and vital marine ecosystems. Here are some stunning beaches across India that you can visit:

RK Beach

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

Ramakrishna Beach is a popular destination in Visakhapatnam known for its stunning coastline and vibrant atmosphere — a favorite for strolls, water sports, and scenic views.

Baga Beach

Goa

Baga Beach is a lively coastal hotspot famous for nightlife, water sports, and scenic beauty — the go-to destination for adventure, beachside shacks, and vibrant Goan culture.

Juhu Beach

Mumbai

One of Mumbai's most iconic beaches, Juhu is beloved for its lively street food scene, sunset views, and the energetic coastal culture that defines the city's spirit.

Gokarna

Karnataka

A serene coastal gem known for its pristine sands, laid-back atmosphere, beach trekking, surfing, and the revered Mahabaleshwara Temple nearby.

Marina Beach

Chennai, Tamil Nadu

One of the longest urban beaches in the world, Marina stretches along the Bay of Bengal and is celebrated for its golden sands, bustling promenade, and breathtaking sunrises.

Varkala

Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala

Also known as Papanasam Beach, Varkala is famous for its dramatic cliffside setting, serene waters, yoga retreats, and some of Kerala's most spectacular sunset views.

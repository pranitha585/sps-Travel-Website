Juhu Beach

Mumbai — Stretching 6km along the Arabian Sea in upscale Juhu neighborhood

Juhu Beach is Mumbai's most iconic beach, famous for its golden sands, celebrity sightings, and mouthwatering street food. Located in the upscale Juhu neighborhood, this beach perfectly captures the spirit of Mumbai — vibrant, energetic, and full of life.

Why Juhu Beach is Special

Iconic Features

  • One of Mumbai's longest beaches (6km)
  • Regular Bollywood celebrity sightings
  • Stunning Arabian Sea sunsets
  • Adjacent to luxury hotels and celebrity homes

Best Experiences

  • Famous street food like bhelpuri and pav bhaji
  • Evening Ferris wheel and amusement rides
  • Long walks along the beach promenade
  • Street performers and live music

Best Time to Visit

  • Early Morning: 5–7am (cool breeze, walkers, yoga groups)
  • Evening: 4–8pm (sunset, street food, entertainment)
  • Best Season: November to February (pleasant weather)
  • Avoid: Monsoon (June–Sept) when beach is messy

Things To Do

Beach Walk & People Watching

Take a long walk along the 6km shoreline while watching:

  • Local cricket matches on the sand
  • Street performers and acrobats
  • Families enjoying picnics
  • Occasional Bollywood shoots

Sunset Photography

Capture stunning shots of:

  • Arabian Sea sunsets
  • Colorful kites during festivals
  • The iconic Juhu skyline
  • Ferris wheel against ocean backdrop

Evening Entertainment

  • Ride the Ferris wheel (₹50-100)
  • Enjoy horse/camel rides (₹100-200)
  • Watch live music performances
  • Shop at beachside stalls

Nearby Attractions

  • ISKCON Temple: Beautiful Hare Krishna temple (1km)
  • Prithvi Theatre: Famous for Hindi plays (2km)
  • Lokhandwala Market: Shopping and cafes (3km)
  • Santacruz Airport: Domestic airport (4km)

Juhu Beach perfectly captures Mumbai's magnetic energy — where Bollywood glamour meets local street life, creating an unforgettable coastal experience in India's maximum city.

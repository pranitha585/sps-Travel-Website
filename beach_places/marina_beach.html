Marina Beach

Chennai — India's longest urban beach stretching 13km along the Coromandel Coast

Marina Beach, the pride of Chennai, is a vibrant stretch of golden sand where the city comes alive. From morning walkers to evening food stalls, this natural urban beach offers a perfect blend of recreation, history, and local culture.

Key Highlights

Iconic Landmarks

  • Mahatma Gandhi Statue — White marble memorial
  • Santhome Basilica — Built over St. Thomas's tomb
  • Triumph of Labor — Famous labor monument
  • University of Madras — Indo-Saracenic architecture

Beach Experiences

  • Sunrise/Sunset views over Bay of Bengal
  • Morning/evening walks on 13km promenade
  • Street food — Murukku, sundal, bhajji
  • Beach games — Giant wheels, balloon shooting

Best Time to Visit

  • Early Morning: 5–7am (cool breeze, sunrise, walkers)
  • Evening: 4–7pm (sunset, street food, activities)
  • Best Season: November to February (pleasant weather)
  • Avoid: Midday heat (10am–3pm) and monsoon (Oct–Dec)

What Makes Marina Beach Special

  • Second longest urban beach in the world (13km)
  • Unique combination of historical landmarks and beach activities
  • Cultural melting pot showcasing Tamil Nadu's diversity
  • Free public space enjoyed by all sections of society

Must-Do Experiences

Landmark Walk

  • Start at Light House (southern end)
  • Walk north past Gandhi Statue, War Memorial
  • See Triumph of Labor and Labour Statue
  • End at Marina Swimming Pool (3km walk)
  • Best time: 5-7am or 4-6pm

Beach Activities

  • Horse Ride
  • Balloon Shooting:
  • Beach Photography
  • Bubble Show

Nearby Attractions

  • Fort St. George (2km): East India Company's first fortress
  • Kapaleeshwarar Temple (3km): Dravidian architecture
  • Government Museum (4km): Bronze gallery
  • Valluvar Kottam (6km): Memorial to Tamil poet

Marina Beach is more than just sand and sea — it's Chennai's living room where memories are made, from childhood play to romantic walks and family outings.

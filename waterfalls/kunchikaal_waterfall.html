Kunchikal Falls

India's Highest Waterfall

Kunchikal Falls is the tallest waterfall in India, with a breathtaking cascading drop in the Western Ghats of Karnataka.

Key Facts

Height: 455 meters (1,493 feet) — Highest in India
Type: Tiered (Cascade) Waterfall
River: Varahi River (fed by the Mani Dam)
Location: Near Hulikal village, Shimoga (Shivamogga) district, Karnataka
Best Time to Visit: July to December (Monsoon & Post-Monsoon)
UNESCO Status: Part of the Western Ghats Biodiversity Hotspot

Best Time to Visit

Peak Flow: August to October
Heavy monsoon rains enhance the falls
Off-Season: January to June
Water flow reduces; access may be restricted

What Makes Kunchikal Falls Unique?

  • India's Tallest Waterfall (Higher than Jog Falls & Nohkalikai Falls)
  • Multi-tiered cascades create a stunning visual effect
  • Surrounded by dense forests of the Western Ghats, rich in biodiversity
  • Part of the Varahi Hydroelectric Project – making it a restricted zone

Things to Know Before Visiting

Permission Required: Contact KPCL (Karnataka Power Corporation Ltd.) in advance

No Direct Road Access: Requires a trek (~5 km) from Hulikal village

Guided Tours Recommended: Locals or forest officials can assist

Safety: Slippery terrain; avoid risky climbs near the falls

Photography: Best viewpoints require trekking

Nearby Attractions

Varahi River Backwaters — Scenic reservoir
Kudremukh National Park — About 80 km away
Agumbe Rainforest — "Cherrapunji of the South", ~70 km
Jog Falls — Approximately 150 km

Why is Kunchikal Falls Less Known?

  • Restricted Access (Due to the hydroelectric project)
  • Remote Location (Difficult terrain, limited tourism infrastructure)
  • Less Publicity (Unlike Jog Falls or Dudhsagar)

A hidden gem in the Western Ghats — India's tallest waterfall awaits the adventurous traveler!

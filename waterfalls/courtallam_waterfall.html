Courtallam Falls

The Healing Waters of Tamil Nadu

Courtallam (or Kuttralam) is a group of nine therapeutic waterfalls nestled in the Western Ghats of Tamil Nadu, near the Kerala border. Known as the "Spa of South India", these falls are famous for their mineral-rich waters, believed to have medicinal properties that cure skin ailments, joint pains, and stress.

Key Facts

Location: Tenkasi District, Tamil Nadu
River Source: Chittar River & tributaries
Best Time to Visit: June to September (peak monsoon flow)
Specialty: Natural Ayurvedic baths & massages

The Six Waterfalls of Courtallam

1. Main Falls (Peraruvi)
2. Five Falls (Aintharuvi)
3. Tiger Falls (Puli Aruvi)
4. Honey Falls (Thenaruvi)
5. Old Courtallam Falls
6. Shenbaga Falls

Best Time to Visit

Peak Season: June–September
Heavy water flow
Good Time: October–November
Pleasant weather
Avoid: March–May

Why Visit Courtallam?

  • Natural Ayurvedic Therapy (No chemicals, just pure water!)
  • Less Commercialized than Hogenakkal or Jog Falls
  • Perfect for Families & Elderly (Gentle slopes, therapeutic waters)

Dudhsagar Falls

The Majestic Sea of Milk

Dudhsagar Falls is one of India's most spectacular waterfalls, located on the Mandovi River in the Western Ghats on the border of Goa and Karnataka.

Key Facts

Height: Approximately 310 meters (1,017 feet)
Type: Tiered (Cascade) Waterfall
Best Time to Visit: June to September (monsoon season, when the falls are at their fullest)
Location:
Goa side: Near Collem (Kulem) village, ~60 km from Panaji
Karnataka side: Bhagwan Mahaveer Wildlife Sanctuary & Mollem National Park

Things to Know Before Visiting

Entry Permits: Required for the Bhagwan Mahaveer Wildlife Sanctuary (available at the forest office).

Swimming: Allowed in certain areas, but dangerous during heavy flow.

Jeep Rides: Available from Mollem to the base of the falls (~₹400–500 per person).

Railway Track Trek: Stay alert — trains pass every 30–40 minutes.

Why "Dudhsagar"?

The name means "Sea of Milk" — the gushing white water resembles milk cascading down the mountain.

Nearby Attractions

Mollem National Park — Goa's largest wildlife reserve
Devil's Canyon — scenic gorge near the falls
Tambdi Surla Temple — oldest temple in Goa

A must-visit for nature lovers, trekkers, and adventure seekers!

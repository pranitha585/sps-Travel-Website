WATERFALLS

Waterfalls are natural formations where water plunges from a height, creating breathtaking landscapes and soothing sounds. They vary in size and shape, with many serving as popular tourist destinations and sources of hydroelectric power. Here are some stunning waterfalls across India that you can visit:

DUDHSAGAR

Goa

Dudhsagar Falls, meaning "Sea of Milk," is a breathtaking four-tiered waterfall on the Mandovi River in Goa, cascading from a height of 310 meters. Surrounded by lush greenery, it's a spectacular sight, especially during the monsoon season.

CHACHAI

Madhya Pradesh

Chachai Falls, located in Madhya Pradesh, is one of India's highest waterfalls, plunging from a height of about 130 meters. Formed by the Bihad River, it's a stunning natural wonder, especially during the monsoon season.

JOG FALLS

Karnataka

Jog Falls, located in Karnataka, is one of India's highest waterfalls, plunging from about 253 meters. Created by the Sharavathi River, it transforms into a spectacular sight during the monsoon season.

KUNCHIKAL

Karnataka

Kunchikal Falls, located in Karnataka, is India's highest waterfall, with a total height of about 455 meters. Fed by the Varahi River, it cascades through the Western Ghats, offering a spectacular view amidst lush greenery.

HOGENAKKAL

Tamil Nadu

Hogenakkal Falls, located on the Kaveri River in Tamil Nadu, is often called the "Niagara of India" for its stunning beauty. The cascading waters, surrounded by rocky terrain, make it a mesmerizing destination, especially for coracle rides.

COURTALLAM

Tamil Nadu

Courtallam Falls, located in Tamil Nadu, is famous for its numerous cascading waterfalls, often called the "Spa of South India." The medicinal properties of the water, combined with the scenic beauty, make it a popular tourist destination.

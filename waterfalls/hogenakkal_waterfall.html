Hogenakkal Falls

The "Niagara of India"

Hogenakkal Falls is a stunning natural waterfall on the Kaveri (Cauvery) River, located on the border of Tamil Nadu and Karnataka. Often called the "Niagara Falls of India", it is famous for its rock formations, coracle rides, and therapeutic oil massages.

Key Facts

Type: Cataract & segmented waterfall (multiple channels)
Height: Approximately 20 meters (66 feet) — not very tall, but wide & powerful
River: Kaveri (Cauvery) River
Location: Dharmapuri district (Tamil Nadu) near Karnataka border (Chamarajanagar district)

Best Time to Visit

Monsoon (August–November)
Most spectacular, roaring waters
Winter (December–February)
Pleasant weather, good for coracle rides
Summer (March–June)
Low water levels, very hot

Things to Do at Hogenakkal Falls

1. Coracle Boat Ride (Must-Do!)
Round-shaped bamboo boats take you close to the falls. Cost: ₹300–500 per boat (fits 4–6 people). Best experience: early morning or late afternoon.
2. Waterfall Bathing & Swimming
Safe zones are marked; avoid strong currents.
3. Visit Carbonatite Rocks
Rare igneous rock formations found here.

Nearby Attractions

Mettur Dam — Approximately 70 km away
Yercaud Hill Station — About 120 km away
Dharmapuri Temples — Kottai Kovil, Hogenakkal Temple

Why is Hogenakkal Famous?

  • One of India's most unique waterfall experiences
  • Best coracle boat ride in South India
  • Natural oil massages & fish spa
  • Featured in movies like "Raavan" (Mani Ratnam)

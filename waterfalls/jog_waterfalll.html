Jog Falls

India's Second-Highest Waterfall

Jog Falls (also called Gerusoppe Falls or Joga Falls) is one of India's tallest and most stunning waterfalls, located in the Shivamogga (Shimoga) district of Karnataka.

Key Facts

Height: 253 meters (830 feet) — 2nd highest plunge waterfall in India after Kunchikal Falls
Type: Plunge Waterfall (uninterrupted free fall)
River: Sharavathi River
Best Time to Visit: July to December (peak flow during monsoon & post-monsoon)
UNESCO Status: Part of the Western Ghats UNESCO World Heritage Site

Four Distinct Streams

Raja (King)
Main central fall
Rani (Queen)
Slower, wider fall
Roarer
Loud, turbulent flow
Rocket
Fast, jet-like stream

Best Time to Visit

Monsoon (July–October)
The waterfall is at its most powerful and majestic
Winter (November–February)
Clear views, pleasant weather
Summer (March–June)
Water flow reduces, but still scenic

Things to Know Before Visiting

Trekking: The 1,400-step descent to the base is steep but rewarding.

Safety: Avoid slippery rocks; swimming is not allowed due to strong currents.

Nearby Attractions

Linganamakki Dam — 25 km from Jog Falls
Tavarekoppa Lion & Tiger Safari — about 50 km away
Ikkeri & Keladi Temples — historical sites nearby

Why is Jog Falls Famous?

  • One of the highest untiered waterfalls in India
  • Featured in movies like "Mungaru Male" (Kannada film)
  • Electricity Generation: The Sharavathi Hydroelectric Project powers parts of Karnataka

A breathtaking spectacle of nature — a must-visit destination in Karnataka!

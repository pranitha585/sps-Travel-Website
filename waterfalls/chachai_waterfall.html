Chachai Falls

Madhya Pradesh's Crown Jewel

Chachai Falls is one of the most stunning waterfalls in Madhya Pradesh, India. It is the highest waterfall in the state and a breathtaking natural attraction.

Key Facts

Height: Approximately 130 meters (427 feet)
Type: Plunge Waterfall (water falls vertically without touching rocks)
Location: Near Rewa district, Madhya Pradesh
River Source: Bihad River (a tributary of the Tamsa River)
Best Time to Visit: July to October (monsoon season, when the water flow is strongest)

Things to Know Before Visiting

Entry Fee: Minimal or no entry fee (check local updates)

Trekking: A short walk (~1 km) from the nearest road point

Safety: Avoid getting too close to the edge; rocks can be slippery

Why "Chachai"?

Chachai Falls is named after the nearby village of Chachai. It is fed by the Bihad River and cascades dramatically off the Rewa Plateau, creating a spectacular sight, especially during the monsoon season.

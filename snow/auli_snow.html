Auli

Uttarakhand's premier skiing destination, nestled in the Garhwal Himalayas at 2,500–3,050m

Auli is India's best-kept skiing secret, with pristine slopes, breathtaking views of Nanda Devi (India's second highest peak), and world-class skiing infrastructure. This Himalayan jewel offers powder snow that lasts from December to March.

Key Highlights

Winter Sports

  • Skiing on 10km slopes with 500m vertical drop
  • Asia's longest (4km) and highest gondola ride
  • Snowboarding on well-groomed runs
  • Tobogganing and sledding for beginners

Summer Attractions

  • Cable car rides with panoramic Himalayan views
  • Trekking to Gorson Bugyal and beyond
  • Sunrise views of Nanda Devi range
  • Lush green meadows (bugyals) in summer

Unique Features

  • Surrounded by coniferous forests and oak trees
  • Artificial snow-making capability
  • Hosts national and international skiing competitions
  • Ski-in ski-out accommodations available

Best Time to Visit

  • Ski Season: December to March (snow depth 2–3m)
  • Best Snow: January–February (consistent powder)
  • Summer: April–June (10–25°C, wildflowers, trekking)
  • Monsoon: July–September (avoid due to landslides)

What Makes Auli Special?

  • Longest cable car in Asia (4.15km) with 10 towers
  • Natural bowl shape prevents avalanches
  • North-facing slopes retain snow longer
  • Stunning views of 7 Himalayan peaks including Nanda Devi

Visitor Tips

Pack proper winter gear – temperatures drop to -10°C
Beginners should take lessons from certified instructors
Carry cash – limited ATMs and card facilities
Check weather and avalanche warnings before skiing
High UV protection needed – snow reflects 80% of UV rays

Skiing Information

Ski Slopes

  • Beginner: 200m gentle slope near Auli Resort
  • Intermediate: 1.5km run from top of chairlift
  • Advanced: 3–4km runs with steeper gradients
  • Cross-country: 10km route to Gorson Bugyal

Nearby Attractions

  • Joshimath (16km) – Base town with Adi Shankara math
  • Valley of Flowers (50km) – UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Badrinath (60km) – Sacred Hindu temple
  • Hemkund Sahib (75km) – Sikh pilgrimage site

Auli offers an unparalleled Himalayan experience – from carving fresh tracks on pristine slopes to meditating on panoramic mountain views, this skiing paradise captivates adventure seekers and nature lovers alike.

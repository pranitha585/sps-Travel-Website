Shimla

Himachal Pradesh's capital city, perched at 2,205m in the Himalayan foothills

Shimla, the former summer capital of British India, enchants visitors with its colonial-era architecture, panoramic mountain views, and crisp mountain air. The ridge-top town offers a perfect blend of heritage charm and modern amenities amidst pine-covered hills, making it one of India's most beloved hill stations.

Key Highlights

Heritage Attractions

  • Viceregal Lodge – British-era summer residence with Scottish baronial architecture
  • Kalka-Shimla Toy Train – UNESCO World Heritage route with 103 tunnels
  • Christ Church – Second oldest church in North India (built 1857)
  • The Mall Road – Colonial-era promenade lined with shops and cafes

Natural Wonders

  • Jakhu Hill – Highest point in Shimla with historic Hanuman temple
  • Annandale – Picturesque picnic spot surrounded by deodar forest
  • Kufri – Snow point offering winter sports and Himalayan views
  • Scandal Point – Famous sunset viewpoint where two valleys meet

Unique Experiences

  • Shimla Skyway – Cable car ride to Jakhu Temple with aerial city views
  • Shopping for wooden handicrafts at Lakkar Bazaar
  • Authentic Himachali cuisine (Dham, Siddu, Chha Gosht) at local eateries
  • Heritage walks through colonial buildings, churches, and gothic structures

Best Time to Visit

  • Summer: April–June (15–30°C, ideal escape from plains heat)
  • Monsoon: July–September (lush greenery, misty mornings, occasional showers)
  • Winter: December–February (-5 to 10°C, occasional snowfall, magical atmosphere)

What Makes Shimla Special?

  • UNESCO-listed Kalka–Shimla Mountain Railway with 103 tunnels and 864 bridges
  • Well-preserved colonial architecture, including Gothic, Tudor, and Scottish baronial styles
  • Stunning Himalayan views from multiple vantage points (Jakhu Hill, The Ridge)
  • Gateway to other Himachal destinations like Manali, Kullu, and Kinnaur

Visitor Tips

Carry layers – evenings get chilly even during summer months
Book Kalka-Shimla Toy Train tickets 2–3 months in advance (peak season)
Start early to avoid crowds at popular spots like The Ridge and Jakhu Temple

Must-Visit Attractions

Viceregal Lodge (Indian Institute of Advanced Study)

  • Former summer residence of British Viceroys, built 1884–1888
  • Beautiful Scottish baronial architecture with oak paneling

Kalka-Shimla Toy Train (UNESCO Heritage)

  • 96km scenic journey with 103 tunnels and 864 bridges
  • 5–6 hour journey through stunning Himalayan foothills

Kufri Excursion

  • 16km from Shimla (about 45 minutes by road)
  • Winter sports available December–February (snow tubing, skiing)

Nearby Getaways

  • Chail (45km) – Former royal retreat with world's highest cricket ground (2,250m)
  • Tattapani (55km) – Natural hot springs by the Sutlej River
  • Solan (40km) – Mushroom capital of India with Shoolini Mata Temple
  • Mashobra (13km) – Quiet forest retreat with presidential retreat "The Retreat"

Shimla remains India's most iconic hill station, where colonial heritage meets Himalayan grandeur. Whether you're chasing snow, heritage, or simply serenity, Shimla welcomes you year-round.

Mussoorie

Uttarakhand's beloved hill station, perched at 2,005m in the Garhwal Himalayas

Mussoorie, with its emerald hills and panoramic Himalayan vistas, has enchanted travelers for generations. Commanding snow ranges to the northeast and glittering views of the Doon Valley to the south, this charming hill resort was once the summer retreat of British officials and remains one of India's most beloved mountain getaways.

Key Highlights

Iconic Attractions

  • The Mall Road – Heart of Mussoorie with shops, cafes, and colonial charm
  • Cable Car to Gun Hill – Second highest peak in Mussoorie
  • Kempty Falls – Famous tiered waterfall perfect for picnics

Nature Spots

  • Lal Tibba – Highest point with telescope views of Himalayan peaks
  • Company Garden – Beautiful floral displays and boating
  • Bhatta Falls – Picturesque picnic spot with natural pools
  • Cloud's End – Tranquil forest walk with spectacular sunsets

Unique Experiences

  • Visiting Ruskin Bond's favorite haunts in Landour
  • Shopping for woolens and handicrafts at Camel's Back Road market
  • Heritage walks through colonial architecture and churches

Best Time to Visit

  • Summer: April–June (15–30°C, ideal escape from heat)
  • Monsoon: July–September (misty landscapes, lush greenery)
  • Autumn: October–November (clear skies, pleasant weather, best views)
  • Winter: December–February (0–15°C, occasional snowfall)

What Makes Mussoorie Special?

  • Stunning views of Himalayan peaks like Bandarpoonch and Srikantha
  • Rich literary connections (Ruskin Bond's beloved hometown)
  • Perfect blend of colonial charm and modern amenities
  • Gateway to Yamunotri and Gangotri pilgrimage sites

Visitor Tips

Wear comfortable shoes – Mussoorie has many steep walks and staircases
Carry light woolens even in summer – evenings get chilly
Visit popular spots early morning to avoid crowds
Network connectivity can be patchy in remote areas like Landour
Carry a water bottle – staying hydrated helps with the altitude

Must-Visit Attractions

Gun Hill

  • Second highest point in Mussoorie (2,024m)
  • Spectacular 180° Himalayan views including Bandarpoonch peak
  • Historic cannon fired during British era to announce time

Kempty Falls

  • 40m high waterfall located 15km from Mussoorie
  • Best visited April–June and September–November

Landour

  • Quaint cantonment area 6km from Mussoorie town
  • Explore Char Dukan (four shops) and Kellogg's Church

Nearby Excursions

  • Surkanda Devi Temple (34km) – Sacred peak with panoramic Himalayan views
  • Yamunotri (130km) – Source of the Yamuna River, one of Char Dham sites
  • Dehradun (35km) – Capital city with museums, institutes, and Robber's Cave
  • Mussoorie Lake (6km) – Man-made lake with boating and sunset views

Mussoorie remains one of India's most beloved hill stations, where misty mountains, colonial-era architecture, and literary heritage create an enchanting getaway throughout the year.

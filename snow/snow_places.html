SNOW REGIONS

Snow regions are areas with frequent snowfall and cold temperatures, typically found in high-altitude or polar zones. Here are some stunning snow destinations across India that you can visit:

AULI

Uttarakhand

Auli is a breathtaking hill station in Uttarakhand, India, known for its snow-covered slopes and skiing adventures. It offers stunning views of the Himalayas and serves as a gateway to trekking destinations like Gorson Bugyal.

GULMARG

Jammu & Kashmir

Gulmarg is a scenic hill station in Jammu and Kashmir, India, known for its lush meadows and world-class skiing slopes. It features the Gulmarg Gondola, one of the highest cable cars in the world, offering breathtaking views of the Himalayas.

MANALI

Himachal Pradesh

Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh, India, famous for its snow-capped mountains and adventure sports. It's a popular destination for skiing, trekking, and exploring scenic spots like Solang Valley and Rohtang Pass.

SHIMLA

Himachal Pradesh

Shimla, the capital of Himachal Pradesh, is a charming hill station known for its colonial architecture and scenic landscapes. It's a popular destination for snowfall, adventure activities, and attractions like Mall Road and Kufri.

MUSSOORIE

Uttarakhand

Mussoorie is a picturesque hill station in Uttarakhand, India, known for its colonial charm and stunning views of the Himalayas. It's famous for attractions like Kempty Falls, Mall Road, and Gun Hill, making it a popular getaway for nature lovers.

LEH LADAKH

Ladakh

Leh-Ladakh, located in northern India, is known for its breathtaking landscapes, high-altitude passes, and Buddhist monasteries. It's a paradise for adventure enthusiasts, offering activities like trekking, biking, and river rafting amid the stunning Himalayas.

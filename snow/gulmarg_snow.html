Gulmarg

Kashmir's premier ski destination, located 52km from Srinagar at 2,650m altitude — nestled in the Pir Panjal range

Gulmarg, meaning "Meadow of Flowers", transforms into a winter paradise from December to March, offering some of the finest skiing and snowboarding terrain in Asia. With the world’s second-highest gondola, breathtaking Himalayan panoramas, and authentic Kashmiri hospitality, this jewel of the Pir Panjal range delivers an unforgettable alpine experience.

Key Highlights

Winter Sports

  • Skiing on Asia's longest ski runs (5km+ terrain)
  • World’s highest gondola — two-phase cable car up to 3,980m
  • Snowboarding at Kongdoori slope with deep powder

Summer Attractions

  • World’s highest green golf course (18 holes, 2,650m)
  • Trekking to Alpather Lake and scenic wildflower meadows
  • Gondola rides with 360° Himalayan views in pleasant weather

Unique Features

  • Natural bowl slopes with reliable powder snow
  • Authentic Kahwa tea in snow-covered wooden huts
  • Stunning sunrise views from Apharwat Peak

Best Time to Visit

  • Ski Season: December to March (heavy snowfall, 3–4m depth)
  • Best Snow & Powder: January–February (ideal for skiing & snowboarding)
  • Summer: June–September (wildflowers, golf, pleasant 10–25°C)
  • Autumn: October–November (crystal-clear mountain views, fewer crowds)

What Makes Gulmarg Special?

  • Home to the world's second-highest gondola (Phase 2 at 3,980m)
  • Powder snow widely regarded as best in the Himalayas outside Europe
  • Longest ski runs in India with heli-skiing opportunities
  • Rich Kashmiri culture, warm hospitality, and legendary meadows

Visitor Tips

Pack proper winter gear – temperatures can drop to -10°C or lower
Beginners should take lessons from certified ski instructors (available at Gulmarg Ski School)
Check weather and avalanche forecasts before any off-piste activity

Adventure & Activities

Skiing & Snowboarding

  • Best slopes: Apharwat Peak, Kongdoori, and beginner zones near Gulmarg Resort
  • Expert terrain: Heli-skiing available for advanced skiers (March – April)

Summer & Year-Round Highlights

  • Golfing: World's highest green golf course, open May–October
  • Trekking: Alpather Lake (frozen in spring, turquoise in summer)
  • Horse riding & sledding: fun for families on lower slopes
  • Photography: Sunrise/sunset over Pir Panjal range

Nearby Attractions

  • Alpather Lake – 13km trek from Gulmarg; frozen during winter, stunning wild iris blooms in summer
  • Strawberry Valley – serene hiking spot with berry picking (summer)
  • Ningle Nallah – picturesque mountain stream surrounded by pine forests
  • Tangmarg – 14km away; gateway town with local Kashmiri handicraft markets
  • Khilanmarg – short trek from Gulmarg, famous for flower meadows and panoramic views

Gulmarg delivers a magnificent blend of adrenaline-pumping winter sports, serene alpine landscapes, and deep cultural richness.
